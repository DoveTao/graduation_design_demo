# Final S1d5 English Abstract

This work establishes `S1d5` as the current clean exported mainline policy for translation-scale correction under the true T57b multiscale evaluation protocol. A key preliminary finding was that the previously reported `path_ratio≈0.983` baseline was not a valid multiscale result, but a wrapper-default scalar-load mismatch artifact caused by incomplete checkpoint configuration restoration. After fixing the evaluation harness and enforcing `explicit-cfg / unexpected=0`, the true T57b multiscale baseline was redefined as `drift=1.494`, `ATE=9.644`, and `path_ratio=0.496`.

Subsequent audits showed that the failure mode was dominated by translation scale rather than direction-only chaining. In particular, the direction-only path ratio stayed near 1.0, while the metric path ratio collapsed to 0.496, indicating that translation magnitude was the primary bottleneck. Based on this diagnosis, S1d5 adopts a `dt-bucket trajectory scale anchor + rot-only fusion` policy. The method uses three dt buckets, `[0.1,0.3)`, `[0.3,0.5)`, and `[0.5,1.0)`, with effective factors `1.302368`, `2.600361`, and `3.966214`, respectively. The final exported policy uses `alpha=1.05`, `fine_rot_fuse_strength=0.40`, `fine_tdir_fuse_strength=0.0`, `fine_tmag_fuse_strength=0.0`, and `use_geometry_refine=False`.

Importantly, the policy was selected by `train_only_leave_one_train_seq_out_cv`, not by test-label fitting. Under the corrected evaluation protocol, S1d5 reproduces `drift=1.396358`, `ATE=7.632463`, `path_ratio=0.934982`, `RPE_trans_mag=0.073926`, with `selected_k=1`, `num_pairs=132`, `num_chains=19`, and `unexpected=0`. Compared with the true T57b multiscale baseline, S1d5 repairs the scale collapse (`0.496 -> 0.935`) while also improving ATE (`9.644 -> 7.632`) and slightly improving drift (`1.494 -> 1.396`).

The result should still be interpreted carefully. S1d5 is an exported calibration/evaluation policy rather than a fully internalized end-to-end learned model, and its dt-bucket factors may require broader validation on more scenes and sequences. Historical scalar-load baselines remain invalid as true baselines. Future work may investigate a minimal trainable dt-anchor head, but such a direction has not been run in the current study.
